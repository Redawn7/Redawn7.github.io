# Product

## Current Visual Direction (2026-10-22)

Visitors browse a focused academic portfolio on a laptop or phone in daylight. Combine Apple's visual clarity with a compact academic index: pure white reading surfaces, a neutral cool-gray identity rail, graphite text, native Apple system typography, and restrained blue links. Retain the fixed identity rail and bilingual content. Use upright type, generous space between sections, and tightly grouped records. Controls are rectangular. Do not use dark or cream backgrounds, italic headline emphasis, section numerals, monospace labels, pill buttons, or large gradient compositions. Motion is limited to brief opacity and color feedback.

## Register

brand

## Users

The homepage serves prospective collaborators, researchers, developers, recruiters, peers, and broadly curious visitors encountering Luxuan Chen for the first time. They need to understand who Luxuan is before deciding whether to explore research, publications, projects, or contact links.

## Product Purpose

Create a concise, memorable personal brand surface that presents identity first and work second. The initial release demonstrates the future structure with disclosed fictional academic information, while making replacement with verified biography, affiliations, publications, and links straightforward.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, and intellectually curious. The voice is direct and human, with academic credibility conveyed through clarity and restraint rather than institutional ornament.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a template marketplace portfolio, a card-filled SaaS dashboard, a magazine cover, or a futuristic AI landing page. Avoid oversized hero slogans, neon or gradient treatments, glassmorphism, generic icon grids, decorative metrics, and visual claims that make fictional data appear verified.

## Design Principles

1. Identity before inventory: establish the person, focus, and contact path before listing achievements.
2. Scan before drill-down: papers and projects should be legible as editorial rows without opening detail pages.
3. Quiet confidence: use typography, alignment, and spacing to communicate quality; decoration is rare and purposeful.
4. Truth is part of the interface: fictional or unverified claims remain visibly disclosed and never link to unrelated real sources.
5. One page, two languages: Chinese and English carry equivalent meaning and preserve the same hierarchy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion preferences, 200% zoom, semantic landmarks, meaningful alt text, 44-pixel touch targets, and a mobile-first reading order. Language switching updates the document language and accessible labels.
