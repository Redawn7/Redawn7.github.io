01

Research

Language models

Verifiable Scientific Reasoning and Mechanistic Interpretability

I study how language models form multi-step reasoning, mathematical abstractions, and causal representations. Using causal interventions, circuit analysis, and formal verification, I work to distinguish faithful reasoning from plausible narration.

AI for physics

Autonomous AI Scientists for Fundamental Physics

I build research agents that read literature, generate hypotheses, call symbolic and numerical tools, analyze evidence, and revise research plans—with a physicist kept firmly in the loop.

Quantum matter

Foundation Models for Quantum Matter and Lattice Field Theory

I use neural quantum states, symmetry-preserving generative models, and differentiable simulators to study wave functions, quantum phase transitions, and non-perturbative dynamics across families of physical systems.
